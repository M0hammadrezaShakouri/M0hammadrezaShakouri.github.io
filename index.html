Summary

I am a Computer Engineering graduate of Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman , and I am about to begin my master's degree at University of Tehran.

My research journey began during my bachelor's studies, where, under the guidance of Professor Mahdi Eftekhari, I worked on self-supervised learning for medical image analysis. This led to DINO-CXR, a vision-transformer-based self-supervised method for chest X-ray classification, published at ISVC 2023. It tackles the scarcity of labeled medical data by learning from unlabeled images first.

Today, my interests center on multimodal learning with vision-language models, trustworthy AI, and AI for healthcare. I am curious by nature and enjoy exploring new research directions instead of staying within one fixed area. I am committed to applying what I learn to meaningful real-world problems, both through teamwork and independent work, and I look forward to engaging with international research groups and taking part in the wider research community.

Publications

DINO-CXR: A Self Supervised Method Based on Vision Transformer for Chest X-Ray Classification

Springer, ISVC 2023

DINO-CXR adapts DINO, a self-supervised learning framework originally built for natural images, to a vision transformer backbone for chest X-ray classification. It tackles a real bottleneck in medical imaging — the scarcity of labeled data — by learning strong representations from unlabeled X-rays first. On pneumonia and COVID-19 detection, it outperforms existing self-supervised methods in accuracy while needing far less labeled data to fine-tune.
